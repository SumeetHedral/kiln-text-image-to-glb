Kiln GLB Gallery

Describe an object or drop an image to get a 3D model in your browser, or browse assets generated with Kiln. Compare WebGL and WebGPU with live metrics.

Text to 3D runs entirely on your device: SD-Turbo makes an image (WebGPU with shader-f16 needed; 2.5 GB one-time download, cached), then Depth Anything V2 turns it into a relief. It is a front-facing relief, not a full object. You can also paste an image.
